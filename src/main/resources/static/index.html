Welcome to the Digital Painting Competition with a Cause!

Our artist foundation is organizing this digital painting competition with a very special purpose: raising funds to buy school supplies for schools in small towns. By participating, you not only showcase your artistic talent but also directly support the education of hundreds of children.

Evaluation Process

The competition will be evaluated by a three-member jury. Each judge will rate the paintings from one to three stars (1 star = not good enough, 2 stars = good, 3 stars = outstanding) and provide comments to justify their decision. To guarantee maximum transparency and fairness, the chairman of the jury will validate every evaluation using a blind signature, ensuring the votes are legitimate without seeing their actual content. At the end of the competition, all evaluations will be publicly disclosed and verified. The three artists with the most stars will be crowned our winners!

Requirements to Participate

  • Registration and Consent: Complete the official registration and sign a consent form authorizing the storage and publication of your digital artwork.
  • Artwork Encryption: You must encipher your digital painting file before submitting it to the system to guarantee its integrity.
  • Secure Key Exchange: Share the encrypted file with the jury and securely transmit the decryption key through our platform.
